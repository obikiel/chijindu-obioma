## Logo Direction

### Concept: "The Pipeline"
- Monogram **CEO** (Chijindu Ezekiel Obioma) stylized as a CI/CD pipeline
- The letters connect with arrows/chevrons (`>`) suggesting flow and automation
- Rendered in Electric Cyan on Deep Space background
- Alternate: Terminal prompt style — `~/ceo $` with blinking cursor

### Logo Variations
1. **Full Logo:** `< CEO /> Chijindu Ezekiel Obioma` (code tag style)
2. **Monogram:** `CEO` in pipeline/bracket style `{CEO}`
3. **Favicon:** Single glyph — stylized `C` with circuit-line detail
4. **Terminal Mark:** `ceo@infra:~$` (for dev-focused contexts)

### Logo Usage Rules
- Minimum size: 32px height
- Always use on dark backgrounds (#0A0E17 or #131A2B)
- Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "C" on all sides
- Never stretch, rotate, or apply drop shadows

## Iconography Style
- **Style:** Outlined, 2px stroke, rounded caps
- **Source:** Lucide Icons or Phosphor Icons
- **Color:** Electric Cyan (#00E5FF) for primary, Slate Gray (#64748B) for secondary
- **Tech icons:** Use official logos for Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, Azure at grayscale, accent on hover

## Visual Motifs
- **Grid lines** — subtle background grid suggesting infrastructure topology
- **Terminal windows** — code blocks styled as real terminal emulators
- **Pipeline arrows** — `>>>` chevron patterns for section transitions
- **Glow effects** — subtle cyan/green glow behind key elements
- **Dot matrix** — connection nodes suggesting network/cloud architecture

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Attributes
| Attribute    | Description                                        |
|--------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| **Direct**   | No fluff. Say what you mean. Like good documentation.|
| **Confident**| You know your stack. Speak with authority.          |
| **Technical**| Use precise terminology. Your audience gets it.     |
| **Human**    | Approachable despite the tech. Not robotic.         |

### Writing Style
- Short sentences. Active voice.
- Lead with impact: "Reduced deployment time by 80%" not "I worked on optimizing..."
- Use metrics and outcomes wherever possible
- Avoid buzzwords without substance ("synergy", "leverage", "disrupt")

### Elevator Pitch
> "I'm Chijindu — a DevOps Engineer who automates everything. I design and build cloud infrastructure on AWS and Azure using Terraform, containerize with Docker and Kubernetes, and ship reliable CI/CD pipelines that let teams deploy with confidence. Whether it's reducing your deployment time from hours to minutes or architecting zero-downtime systems, I turn infrastructure into a competitive advantage."
